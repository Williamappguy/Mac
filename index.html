Mr Chester.

Mr Chester Wine Bar ◆Fortitude Valley

Tucked away in Fortitude Valley, Mr Chester is the kind of place that makes you wonder why you ever drink anywhere else. Boutique wines, a room that actually has a personality, and just the right amount of intimacy. One drink.

Nº 03 / 05 7:00 PM
7:00PM

Dinner.

Baja Modern Mexican ◆211 Brunswick St, Fortitude Valley

This is where it gets good. Baja is modern Mexican done with real conviction — the kind of place where the kingfish aguachile alone justifies the booking, and the mezcal margaritas make the decision to stay another round very, very easy. We're in no rush. The comedy doesn't start until nine-thirty.

Brisbane Powerhouse ◆Show at 9:30 PM

Damien Power is the kind of comedian who gets you laughing out loud and then, about thirty seconds later, makes you realise the joke was smarter than it first appeared. Three Melbourne Comedy Festival nominations, sold-out rooms, the lot. We'll get there a little early to grab a drink before we head in. Tickets are sorted.
